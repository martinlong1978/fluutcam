# M6 — 2-D and Hole Types Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The first part of M6: the 2-D and hole types of D15b §2 and §3 on the M3 framework. They are `face`, `adaptive_2d`, `trace`, `engrave`, `chamfer_2d`, `bore` and `drill`. Each type gets its parameters, validation, strategy, a golden G-code reference project and a translation-invariance test.

**Architecture:**
- `fluut-model`:
  - a field table, a resolved struct and the D15b §2–§3 validation rules for each type;
  - a `Duration` field kind (D15b §1.1);
  - tool additions: `shape = "drill"`, `point_angle_deg`, `pitch`, and `optimal_load` in presets. V tools also need a half-angle and a tip diameter (see Scope decisions);
  - `PostCaps` learns about helices and canned cycles.
- `fluut-cam`:
  - one strategy module per type in `strategy/`;
  - the S3 adaptive spike promoted to `strategy/adaptive_2d.rs`, with the exact engagement code in `engage.rs`;
  - shared hole finding (`holes.rs`) for `drill` and `bore`.
  
  Framework edits are kept small:
  - `StrategyInput.stock`: the stock box in W, used by `face` and by open pockets in `adaptive_2d`;
  - a stay-down link is checked against the next cut's region only;
  - `wall_pieces` can take top edges as well as bottom edges.
- `fluut-core`: fills `StrategyInput.stock` and the new `PostCaps` fields. At post time, a tap cycle feeds at rpm × pitch.
- `fluut-cli`: reference projects with hand-written STEP parts, golden G-code, and translation invariance for every new project.

**Tech Stack:** as M3. No new crates.

**Design:** [D15b](../design/D15b-toolpath-parameters-2.md) §1–§3, §9; [D15](../design/D15-toolpath-parameters.md) §1, §2, §7; [D09](../design/D09-strategies.md) §2, §3; [D08](../design/D08-toolpath-framework.md); [D10](../design/D10-post-engine.md) §5; [S3 report](spikes/s3-region-2d.md); M3 plans [vertical slice](2026-11-01-m3-vertical-slice.md) and [parallel](2026-11-01-m3-parallel.md).

## Scope decisions

- **In-process stock** (D08 §7) does not exist yet. The types do not update stock, and `rest_from = "previous"` stays the error `param.geometry.rest_from.unsupported`, as for `pocket_2d`.
- **V tools.** `engrave` and `chamfer_2d` need the half-angle α and the tip radius Rt (D07 §1), but the tool format has no keys for them. This plan adds `half_angle_deg` and `tip_diameter` (default 0) for `v` and `taper` tools to spec 02 §2.4. A `v` or `taper` tool without `half_angle_deg` is not a V tool.
- **Drill tools.** `shape = "drill"` has corner radius 0. `point_angle_deg` defaults to 118.
- **Heights per hole** (D15b §1.6). `drill` and `bore` resolve the heights once for each hole, with `selection` set to that hole's Z range. Parameter validation runs once, on the selection range of all holes.
- **Holes.** A hole is one or more coaxial cylindrical faces whose axis is parallel to Z. Their axes must match within `machining_tolerance`. A face counts as a hole when its material is outside the cylinder (the face normal points to the axis), and as a boss otherwise. A cone face below the deepest cylinder, on the same axis, gives `to_cone_tip` its tip.
- **`face`.** The stock outline is the XY box of the Setup stock. One-way passes (`climb`, `conventional`) do not stay down between passes. They follow `linking.retract` and lift as for any other link.
- **`trace` in `curve` mode.** The Z at a point of the path is the Z of the nearest point, in XY, of the selected curves, sampled at `machining_tolerance`. It is applied to the pass as a Z profile (as for tabs).
- **`chamfer_2d` tip gouge.** A flat cutter of the tip diameter (at least 0.01 mm) is dropped onto the model along the tool-centre path. If it stops above the tip Z by more than the tolerance, the tip would touch the model.
- **`drill` cycles.** The `dwell` cycle is posted as `spot` with its dwell. The `chip_break_retract` length has no cycle parameter in D10 §5, so the post's own gap is used when the cycle is expanded. `retract_mode = "retract"` links between holes at the retract height. The built-in posts use G99, so for native cycles the R plane decides the return height; that is recorded as a follow-up.
- **`adaptive_2d`.** The path is the same at every level, because there is no in-process stock. It is computed once per area and reused, with a ramp only on the first entry of each level. Links between passes stay down when the straight move stays inside the cleared region. The speed target is < 1 s per level on the S3 shapes A, B and C (S3 report §7).
- **`engrave`** needs an exact medial axis of lines and arcs (CH-17). The S3 report estimates 3–5 weeks for a robust version. If there is no time for it in this plan, `engrave` gets its parameters and validation only, and `generate` reports `cam.type.unavailable`, as `parallel` did in M3.

**Done.** `face`, `adaptive_2d`, `trace`, `chamfer_2d`, `bore` and `drill` are built on the M3
framework, each with its D15b parameters and validation, tests, and a reference project:
- `plate2d` (`crates/fluut-cli/tests/projects/plate2d/`; renamed from `plate` at merge, since M7 has a `plate` project) runs `face`, `adaptive_2d`, `trace` and
  `chamfer_2d`, posted with `fluut/linuxcnc`;
- `holes` runs `bore` and `drill`, posted with `fluut/linuxcnc` (native peck cycles) and
  `fluut/grbl` (cycles expanded into moves). A tap test posts with a custom post that has a tap
  cycle.

Both projects are translation-invariant: the G-code is identical and every coordinate is within
1e-6 mm. Every D15b §2–§3 example round-trips byte for byte. Every D15b validation code of these
types has a passing and a failing case, except `cam.engrave.flat_area` (below).

**`engrave` is not finished.** It needs the exact medial axis of lines and arcs (CH-17), which
the S3 report estimates at 3–5 weeks. Its parameters and validation are done, and `generate`
checks its drive chains and then reports `cam.type.unavailable`, as `parallel` did in M3.

**`adaptive_2d` speed.** One level on the S3 shapes takes 0.97 s (A), 0.65 s (B) and 1.04 s (C),
against 17, 8.6 and 14 s for the spike. This is `cargo run -p fluut-cam --example adaptive_2d
--release` in a Linux container, one thread. The target was < 1 s, so C is just over it, and
runs vary by about ±20 %. Most of the time is in engagement evaluation (about 17 per step).
The rest is the booleans and offsets of the cleared region.

Deviations from this plan and from the design:
- **V tool keys.** Spec 02 §2.4 gains `half_angle_deg` and `tip_diameter` for `v` and `taper`
  tools. D15b and D07 assume them, but the tool format did not have them.
- **Shared framework edits:**
  - `StrategyInput.stock`;
  - a stay-down link is checked against the next cut's region only (it no longer needs the
    same area index);
  - `wall_edges` gives the top edges of wall faces;
  - `Out::cycle`;
  - `PostCaps` has `helix` and `cycles`;
  - the boolean module's `Indexed` is crate-visible, for the adaptive engine;
  - the test STEP writer keys edges by their curve as well as their ends, so two half circles
    with the same ends stay apart. The existing STEP files are unchanged.
- **`fluut-core`:**
  - it fills the stock box and the new post capabilities;
  - at post time a tap feeds at rpm × pitch;
  - the post-dependent codes of `bore` and `drill` are checked again for cached results.
- **`adaptive_2d` differs from the spike** in three ways:
  - A step whose target heading would leave the tool-centre region follows the region's edge.
    The spike's bisection could step up to a step length into a wall.
  - A rest phase restarts at a fifth, then a twentieth, of the target engagement to take
    slivers.
  - After the final contour, one short move goes into each sliver that is still left.
  
  The engagement RMS error is 18–25° (spike: 12–18°). This is because steps along walls are
  under-engaged by construction.
- **`cam.adaptive_2d.incomplete`** is raised when material thicker than twice the machining
  tolerance is left after the final contour and the sliver moves. Without the sliver moves,
  0.1–0.4 mm² remains per S3 shape, up to 0.36 mm thick.
- **Offsets of the cleared region** go loop by loop: outer loops shrunk, holes grown. If an
  outer loop's offset fails, it is retried up to 0.01 mm less deep. With tiny holes,
  `cavalier_contours` returns only empty loops for the whole region, and one long swept loop
  failed at R − 1e-6 although it offset at R. This is recorded for CH-16.
- **`drill` with native cycles.** The built-in posts write G99, so between holes the tool
  returns to the R plane even with `retract_mode = "retract"`. The toolpath's links at the
  retract height take effect only when cycles are expanded. `chip_break_retract` has no cycle
  parameter in D10 §5 and is not passed to the post. `dwell` posts as `spot` with its dwell.
- **`face` passes** are laid out across the grown area, as D15b says. A pass on the outer edge
  of that area can miss the stock, as in the plate golden file, where it is outside the stock
  by more than R.
- **One-way `face` passes** do not stay down between passes.
- **`trace` in `curve` mode** takes Z from the nearest point, in XY, of all selected curves. A
  Z profile on the pass rules out the `profile` ramp, which falls back to `plunge` with
  `cam.linking.ramp_fallback`.
- **In-process stock** is still not built. No type updates it, and `rest_from = "previous"` is
  still `param.geometry.rest_from.unsupported`.
- **† constants** were not tuned: there were no real cuts in this session.
