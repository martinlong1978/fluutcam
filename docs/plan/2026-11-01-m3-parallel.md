**Goal:** The `parallel` toolpath type (D09 §4.3, D15 §5) on the M3 framework, using the S2 drop queries. This completes M3: a STEP file with curved faces plus a TOML project go to G-code from the CLI, and the translation-invariance test passes for all three types.

**Architecture:**
- `fluut-cam::surface` (new): the Setup's bodies as drop-query geometry.
  - One `QueryGeometry` per B-rep body, without the excluded faces.
  - The drop of the tool with its profile grown by `stock_to_leave` (D07 §1). For a tool of radius R and corner r, a stock s is a drop with radius R + s and corner r + s, lifted by s.
  - The slope at a contact, and whether the contact is on a drive face.
  - A conservative bound of the highest geometry under a link, from the primitive boxes.
- `fluut-cam::scallop` (new): the worst convex and concave curvature of the drive faces, and the stepover for a scallop height.
- `fluut-cam::strategy::parallel`:
  - the region and the pass layout;
  - adaptive sampling along each pass, with exact cut-off points found by bisection;
  - directions, linking and leads.
- `fluut-cam::cutter`: one addition, a `QueryGeometry` built from a subset of a body's faces.
- `fluut-cli`: a reference project with a cylinder face, golden G-code, translation invariance and an end-to-end run.

**Tech Stack:** as M3. No new crates.

## Scope decisions

- **Region.** The tool-centre region is the XY box of the drive faces (from the conservative primitive boxes). A `boundary` then limits it: offset by −R, 0 or +R for `tool_containment`, and intersected (`side = inside`) or subtracted (`outside`), as in `pocket_2d`. The exact drive-face outline (D03 §5.3) waits for M6.
- **What is cut.** A CL point is cut when all of these hold:
  - the contact is on a drive face, or on an edge or vertex of one;
  - its slope is in [`min_slope_deg`, `max_slope_deg`];
  - its tip is not above `top`.
  
  Everything else is linked over. A point with nothing under the tool is at `bottom` with slope 0. CL points below `bottom` are raised to `bottom`.
- **Check faces** are in the query geometry, so the tool never gouges them. Contacts on them are not cut. Excluded faces are left out of the query.
- **Slope** is the angle between +Z and the normal of the tool's lower surface at the contact. For ball and bull tools this is the face normal at face contacts, and it is defined at edges and vertices too. A contact under the flat bottom has slope 0. On the rim of a flat tool, a face contact takes the face normal and an edge or vertex contact counts as 90°.
- **Sampling** (D08 §4.2): adaptive midpoint subdivision until each chord is within `tol/4` of the drop and no longer than R/2. Collinear chords are then merged within `tol/4`, so the output is within `tol/2`. The output is lines only. Arc fitting (D08 §5) and contact transitions (D07 §5) are M6 work.
- **Cut-off points** are found by bisection on the pass parameter to 1e-7 mm. This covers the slope limits, `top`, check faces, the region ends and stay-down links.
- **Scallop stepover** (D09 §4.3). The exact two-ball cusp formula on a sphere of radius ρ = 1/κ_max, with the corner radius r in place of R:
  - plane faces: κ = 0;
  - cylinders and spheres: closed form;
  - other faces: principal curvatures sampled on a 9 × 9 grid over the face's parameter box.
  
  The result is the stepover between contact points. This equals the XY stepover on level ground and is smaller than it on convex ground.
- **Mesh faces.** There is no mesh body type yet. `param.parallel.drive_faces.mesh_scallop` treats a body whose faces are all planar triangles as a mesh.
- **Concave warning.** `cam.parallel.scallop.concave`: a drive face whose concave curvature radius is ≤ R.
- **Linking.**
  - Between passes and over uncut gaps, the tool goes up to a link height, moves across at that height, rapids down to `safety_distance` above the next entry and plunges.
  - `minimum` and `stay_down`: the link height is `safety_distance` above the highest geometry the tool can meet along the link, capped at `retract`.
  - `full`: the link height is `retract`.
  - Neither ever goes below that geometry bound.
  - Between passes, the tool stays down and follows the surface when all of these hold: `stay_down` (any length) or `minimum` (up to one tool diameter); the straight link lies inside the region; and every point of it would be cut.
  - `transitions = smooth` is treated as `direct`, as in the 2-D types.
- **Leads.** `arc` and `linear` leads are vertical, in the plane of the pass. A lead that would gouge (drop above it by more than the tolerance) is dropped, with `cam.parallel.lead.dropped` (W).
- **Bounds.** `SetupGeometry::bounds` takes exact line and arc boxes (`cutter::geom::curve_box`) instead of tessellation, so `model_top` is exact on arcs. It is used for the `parallel` default heights.

## Review Focus

1. **No gouges:** dense drops along every cut and link segment are never above the segment by more than the tolerance (D08 §11).
2. **Exact ends:** pass ends on the region boundary or at a cut-off point, within 1e-6 mm.
3. **Scallop:** the measured cusp on a ball tool is ≤ the requested height.
4. **Stock to leave:** the CL is the drop on the surface offset by s, for s > 0 and −r < s < 0.
5. **Climb, conventional, zig-zag** directions and the side of the next pass (spindle M3).
6. **Translation invariance** on the reference project.
